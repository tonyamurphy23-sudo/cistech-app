The problem

Small agencies lose referrals to slow answers, not bad care.

The referral arrives scattered

A fax, a portal message, a phone call, a PDF. Someone has to pull it together before anyone can decide.

One person wears every hat

With 50 clients or fewer, your admissions person is also scheduling, verifying insurance and answering the phone.

A slow yes looks like a no

By the time clinical review finishes, the discharge planner has already placed the patient with someone who answered first.

How it works

From referral to answer, in one place.

  1. Collect

    Referrals from hospitals, physicians and case managers land in one central inbox, whatever channel they came from.

  2. Organize

    CIS Tech pulls out what matters for a decision: diagnosis, care needs, payer, location and start date.

  3. Match

    Each patient is checked against your agency's profile: the services you offer, the payers you take, your staff and your capacity.

  4. Respond

    Your team sees a clear accept, decline or needs-review recommendation and answers the referral source inside 15 minutes.
